Learn to recognize the signs of phishing attempts before they catch you out. This is a static educational resource — no real emails are sent and no credentials are ever collected.

Common Red Flags in Phishing Emails

  • Urgent or threatening language ("Your account will be suspended in 24 hours")
  • Sender address that looks almost right but isn't (e.g. support@arnazon.com)
  • Generic greetings ("Dear Customer") instead of your actual name
  • Links where the visible text doesn't match the real destination URL
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs, or payment details via email or SMS
  • Unexpected attachments, especially .zip, .exe, or macro-enabled files
  • Poor grammar, spelling mistakes, or inconsistent formatting

Example Walkthrough

From: security-alert@paypa1-support.com
Subject: Urgent: Unusual sign-in detected

"We noticed a login from a new device. Click here to verify your identity within 24 hours or your account will be locked."

Spot the issues: the domain uses a "1" instead of an "l", creates artificial urgency, and pushes you to click a link rather than log in directly through the official site.

What To Do If You Spot One

  • Don't click any links or download attachments
  • Verify by going directly to the official website or app, not via the email link
  • Report it to your IT/security team or email provider's "report phishing" option
  • Delete the message once reported
